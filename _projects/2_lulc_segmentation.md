**Organization:** Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS), Independent University, Bangladesh

**Advisors:** Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman, Dr. Amin Ahsan Ali

Rapidly developing cities such as Dhaka have structural complexities that degrade the performance of even state-of-the-art land use and land cover (LULC) segmentation models. This project proposes a model that incorporates both vision and text modalities: we built a dataset of administrative information and spatial features that, when provided alongside satellite imagery, improves segmentation performance.

This builds on the BOLM high-resolution LULC dataset for Dhaka and my undergraduate thesis on semi-supervised LULC segmentation with Cross Pseudo Supervision.
