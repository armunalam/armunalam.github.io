**Organization:** Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS), Independent University, Bangladesh

**Advisors:** Dr. Md Mofijul Islam, Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman, Aman Chadha

Document understanding systems are dominated by high-resource languages. This project curates a benchmark dataset for low-resource languages, including Bangla, Sinhala, Tamil, and Malayalam, to improve document understanding tasks in a multimodal setting, such as layout grounding, visual question answering, and key information retrieval, and proposes a model architecture for them.

A first outcome of this line of work is BaFCo, a benchmark for complex Bangla form comprehension (ECCV 2026).
