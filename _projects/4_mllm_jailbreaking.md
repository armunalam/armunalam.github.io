**Organization:** Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS), Independent University, Bangladesh

**Advisor:** Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman

This project studies the safety of current closed-source multimodal large language models (MLLMs), looking for loopholes that cause them to produce misaligned and harmful responses, with the goal of informing more robust alignment.
