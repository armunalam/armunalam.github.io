Armun Alam

M.Sc. student at McGill University & Mila – Quebec AI Institute

I am an M.Sc. student at McGill University and Mila – Quebec Artificial Intelligence Institute, supervised by Dr. Samira Abbasgholizadeh-Rahimi.

I am broadly interested in multimodal learning and AI in healthcare. My current work mostly aligns with vision-language models and world models.

Before McGill, I was a Research Assistant at the Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS), Independent University, Bangladesh, where I worked with Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman and Dr. Amin Ahsan Ali, in collaboration with Dr. Md Mofijul Islam and Aman Chadha. My projects there spanned multimodal document understanding for low-resource languages, and vision-and-text land use and land cover segmentation (remote sensing).

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Independent University, Bangladesh, graduating Summa Cum Laude with a CGPA of 3.99/4.00, the highest in the department. I have also worked as an AI Engineer at I-Venture Limited and as a Software Engineer at Cloud Creative Limited.

Outside research, I enjoy programming, playing the piano, and music.
