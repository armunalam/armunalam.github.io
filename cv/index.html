Research Projects

Multimodal Document Understanding for Low-Resource Languages

CCDS · Advisors: Dr. Md Mofijul Islam, Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman, Aman Chadha

Curating a benchmark for Bangla, Sinhala, Tamil, and Malayalam documents covering layout grounding, visual question answering, and key information retrieval in a multimodal setting, and proposing a model architecture.

Vision-Language Land Use and Land Cover Segmentation for Developing Countries

CCDS · Advisors: Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman, Dr. Amin Ahsan Ali

Combining satellite imagery with a dataset of administrative information and spatial features to improve LULC segmentation for structurally complex cities such as Dhaka.

Automatic Exam Script Checking

CCDS · Advisors: Dr. Md Mofijul Islam, Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman, Aman Chadha

Building a benchmark and a language-model approach that predicts marks for computer science exam scripts and explains the reasoning behind the marking.

MLLM Jailbreaking

CCDS · Advisors: Dr. AKM Mahbubur Rahman

Finding loopholes in closed-source multimodal LLMs that lead them to produce misaligned and harmful responses.
